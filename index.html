Planning rules & data settings

Proposed PKK Penampang planning rules. Scope covers fixed food premises. Bazaar, festival and mobile vendors are excluded by operation and address. A documented fixed shop can be retained. Check ambiguous premises with the register officer.

Routine requires a valid latest score at or above the threshold. Needs verification covers missing branch or inspection evidence, conflicting latest scores and recorded permanent closure. Missing coordinates affect mapping only.

Import format

Choose the annual FSQ registers (.xlsx), the preparation workbook, or a CSV. CSV headers: premises, address, year, inspection date, score, area, latitude, longitude. Optional: premises ID, source row, premises type (fixed or temporary). Include the original source row for date corrections. Annual register filenames must contain their reporting year.

Files are read on this device. Refresh or close this page to clear the session. The map uses bundled district tiles. Shared or implausible coordinates are withheld. Named area groups are provisional inspection routes.
